# Code of conduct

This policy applies to participation in CodeMe's issues, pull requests, code reviews, and other project-managed spaces.

Treat people respectfully. Focus criticism on behavior, evidence, and code. Make room for different experience levels and backgrounds. Give clear feedback, accept reasonable correction, and avoid personal attacks, harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual content directed at participants, or repeated unwanted contact.

Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information, credentials, private code, conversations, or purchase records. Do not use project discussions for spam, impersonation, or misleading claims of affiliation.

If a discussion becomes difficult, step back and ask for clarification. Report harmful conduct privately to **support@dhebe.com** where possible, sharing only the details needed to investigate. Do not post sensitive evidence publicly. There is no promised response time.

The maintainer may ask participants to revise content, remove inappropriate material, close a discussion, or restrict participation using the platform's available controls. Decisions should reflect the behavior, context, and effect on other participants. This policy does not replace GitHub's terms or give anyone permission to access private data.
